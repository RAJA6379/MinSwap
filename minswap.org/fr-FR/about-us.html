About us

Cardano’s destination for traders.

Trade your favorite Cardano tokens with low fees, earn yields and stake for real yield.

Swap thousands of tokens on Cardano and access the deepest liquidity on the most decentralized chain.

Minswap Labs is a story of a group of young friends who set out to build a DEX on Cardano, and ended up building up a global company of experienced marketers, designers, crypto natives and engineers. Our flagship product, the Minswap DEX has held the number 1 position for Cardano Volumes for more than 3 years. It won the Cardano Summit Best DeFi awards in 2022 and 2023, and Best DEX and DAO Tooling & Governance awards in 2024.

Our mission

We aim to bring the best decentralized trading experience and passive income to anyone in the world.

Our values

Constantly listening and involving our community in every step we took. Pioneer novel DeFi ideas and push the boundaries of Cardano blockchain technology to the limit, while maintaining transparency and integrity as true buidlers.
